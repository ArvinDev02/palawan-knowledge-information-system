About the E-Library

Palawan Council for Sustainable Development

The Palawan Council for Sustainable Development (PCSD) is the governing body for coordinated and integrated planning and management of the province's natural resources. Established under Republic Act No. 7611, PCSD sets the strategic direction for balancing economic development with environmental protection.

Our E-Library is a digital repository of government publications, research reports, and strategic documents. It serves researchers, policymakers, students, and citizens who need access to reliable, authoritative information on sustainable development in Palawan.

🛡️

Official Repository

All publications are verified and approved by PCSD governance bodies.

Trusted Source

Our Mission

To provide free, equitable access to government knowledge and research on sustainable development, empowering individuals and organizations to make informed decisions that protect Palawan's natural resources.

Our Vision

A Palawan province where government information is transparent, accessible, and used by all stakeholders to drive sustainable environmental and social progress.

The E-Library Platform

Launched in 2021, our digital library has grown to house over 5,000 publications across 15 languages. We continuously expand our collection to serve researchers, students, policymakers, and the general public.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the E-Library really free?
›
Yes, completely free! All publications are public resources funded by government. No registration, subscription, or payment required.
Can I download publications for offline use?
›
Absolutely. You can download most publications as PDF, EPUB, or plain text. Check each publication page for available formats.
Are these publications in languages other than English?
›
Yes! We have publications in Filipino, Tagalog, Cebuano, Palawano, and 10+ other regional languages. Use the language filter to find them.
How often is the collection updated?
›
We add new publications every week. Subscribe to our newsletter to get notified about new releases in your areas of interest.
Can I cite these publications in academic work?
›
Yes! Each publication includes a suggested citation format on its detail page. All content is public domain or Creative Commons licensed.
Do you have a mobile app?
›
Our web app is fully responsive and works great on phones. A native iOS/Android app is now available.
