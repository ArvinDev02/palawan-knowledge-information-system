About PCSD E-Library

Stewards of Palawan's Knowledge

The Palawan Council for Sustainable Development (PCSD) is the primary government agency mandated by Republic Act 7611 — the Strategic Environmental Plan for Palawan Act — to oversee the sustainable development and conservation of Palawan's natural resources and environment.

Recognized globally as the Philippines' Last Ecological Frontier, Palawan is home to extraordinary biodiversity, including countless endemic species found nowhere else on Earth. The PCSD E-Library serves as the official digital repository of the agency's decades-long body of research, policy work, and scientific documentation.

Through this platform, researchers, students, LGU officials, and the public gain free access to a comprehensive collection of environmental laws, biodiversity studies, PCSD issuances, and Palawan-focused research — supporting evidence-based decision-making across the region.

THE DIGITAL ARCHIVE

About the E-Library

The PCSD E-Library was established as part of the agency's commitment to transparency, public service, and the democratization of environmental knowledge. Under its digitization mandate, PCSD has systematically converted decades of printed documents, research reports, and official issuances into accessible digital formats.

The collection spans over three decades of environmental governance in Palawan — from the landmark passage of RA 7611 in 1992 to the present. It includes the full text of legislation such as the Wildlife Resources Conservation Act (RA 9147), the Chainsaw Act (RA 9175), and the Indigenous Peoples Rights Act (IPRA), alongside PCSD-specific resolutions, administrative orders, and technical studies.

Public access is a core principle of the E-Library. All documents in the collection are freely available to browse and download. Registered users gain access to additional features including saved search history, reading lists, and personalized research recommendations.

FREQUENTLY ASKED

Common Questions

How do I register for an account?
Registration is free. Click the "Login" button in the navigation bar, then select "Create a Free Account." You will need a valid email address. After verifying your email, you can immediately begin using all E-Library features.
Are all publications free to download?
Yes. All publications in the PCSD E-Library are freely available for download as PDF files. As official government documents, they are in the public domain. Some restricted research reports may require a registered account to access.
What laws and issuances are available?
The E-Library includes full texts of RA 7611 (SEP for Palawan Act), RA 9147 (Wildlife Resources Conservation Act), RA 9175 (Chainsaw Act), IPRA, and all PCSD resolutions and administrative orders from 1992 to the present.
Can I access the E-Library without an account?
Yes. Browsing and reading publications online is available to all visitors without registration. An account is only required to download files, save research lists, or access restricted collections.
How often is the collection updated?
The collection is updated quarterly, with new PCSD issuances added as they are released. New research papers and external publications are catalogued on a rolling basis by our library staff.
Who can I contact for research assistance?
For specialized research queries, contact the PCSD Information and Documentation Unit at info@pcsd.gov.ph or call (048) 433-2824 local 208. Office hours are Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM PHT.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked

How do I report a broken link or missing publication?
Use the contact form above and select "Technical Support" from the subject dropdown. Describe the publication title and the issue you encountered. Our team will follow up within 2 business days.
Can I request a physical copy of a PCSD publication?
Physical copies of select publications may be available at the PCSD main office in Puerto Princesa City. Contact us directly to check availability and make arrangements for in-person pickup.
How do I request a publication not in the collection?
Use the contact form and choose "Publication Request." Provide the full title, author, and publication year. Our library staff will work to locate or digitize the material if it falls within PCSD's collection scope.
I'm a researcher — can PCSD provide data for my study?
Yes. PCSD supports scientific research through its Information and Documentation Unit. Submit a "Research Request" via the contact form, including your institutional affiliation and specific data needs. Requests are reviewed on a case-by-case basis.
